Goa !!

One of the best tours I had ever went and you should go to !!

This was a 5 day school trip to goa and the total package was ₹13,500

Day 1 -

The travelling day:

On this day more than 100 students waited excitedly in the Gnanapuram railway station for the train to arrive.
The train arrived at the railway station at 12:36 p.m. which was coming from Shalimar to Vasco Da Gama. I boarded the train with all others and went to my seat.Once everyone had settled, the train started to move. All the fathers drove out the passengers without reserved seats and gave us all the safety instructions.
In the first hour everyone was settled and the first thing we did was we shouted "Skimbleshanks", Skimbleshanks is a fictional character in a story who was a very disciplined Railway cat, this story was taught to us last month so that's why.
In the whole train journey we were howling when tunnels, dancing singing, crushing the bags and your imagination is your limit. Then from the speaker that one song came and the whole compartment shook, the one and only "jim jim tara jim tara jalebi tera chinnoda, jalebi tera chinnoda manam jaldigi podam chinnoda". Damn that was fascinating to watch. Then I remembered that I brought muri mixture which was a very loved snack of Indians, and my friends had a very fun way of mixing it. We then after ate our dinner and slept according to our berths.
If you are curious this(18047) is our train number.

Day 2 -

Train journey + hotel check in:

Today I had woke up at 2:30 a.m., this was because everyone kept their phones into one locked bag for safety and my friends wanted to play games so they gave everyone their phones and me who does not want to play they woke me up and they played at 2:30 a.m. in the morning.
Then at 6:00 a.m. I did my brush and fortunately there was no faecal matter excretion in the train, because the bathrooms in the trains were the worst.
Then shortly we had breakfast in the train which none liked much and just threw what they don't like out of the train, but I eat my breakfast because it was kinda ok and I was taught not to waste food. Then at nearly 10 or 11 a.m. we had arrived Madgaon(a city in Goa), this is where we were going to stay.
Everyone jumped down the train and then we got to know that there was again 2 hour bus ride to our Hotel Citrus, the hotel name is citrus but the lunch did not have any citrus fruits lol. After keeping our suitcases in the back of the bus we went into the bus and arrived at the hotel and checked in.
The accommodation was very nice, but the floors were always moistury and the T.V. was put for namesake and was excited when I was going into the pool. Then after bathing and everything was done we went down for our Lunch which was actually very good, it was a buffet.
Then we had gone out to Candolim beach, its was a 2km walk from the hotel and it was not much exciting just for seeing and there were mostly parties going on. We were not able to touch the water because it was too dark. Then return to hotel and sleep.

Day 3 -

Exploring and Adventure:

Today morning after breakfast we had went to Agoda fort for sight seeing, the place was nice and decent to take a few photos. Also even after getting provided with water bottles all of our water was exhausted so everyone and I too felt it so me and my group after quickly completing it had went out and bought cool water bottles and back to the bus to the next destination.
And then afterwards we had went out for Baga beach, this is the best beach I had ever seen in my life, it had no waste anywhere and the point where the river flowing into the Arabian Sea and the water level only rising upto half feet was so perfect and so good that even words cannot explain it. Eve the sand unlike other beaches which was good and had easily fallen off smoothly just by a gentle touch the water made everything fabulous.
Then at 6:00 p.m. there was a dance party for our school in the hotel melody hall. Everyone danced like crazy, the students who shook their bodies violently to the people who knew dance all danced and I too danced or I did not.

Day 4 -

Extreme Exploration and Adventure:

Today after waking up and was seen peeped through the broken window directly in the bathroom, we had gone to a church known as Ballaca, it was an Old Church and had a history full of knowledge, when I went inside it was soooooo quiet and I slid some charity amount in the donation box too and wrote a wish too.
Then we had gone to Mangeshri temple, which was nice but I did not go inside but my friends say that their feet had burnt as they had to walk without shoes or slippers. I had took photos with a cow which seemed interesting to me.
Then for lunch we had gone to a random place we were not told and we ate and the food was ok.
We then went to Dona Paula which used to be a beach ig but as the water had rised a lot it was constructed into a view point. Anyways got a few colab photos there and cool to the see the crabs present there.
And thennnnnn the cruiseferry boat, this was soo cool, I got the most photos and videos there, the ship cutting through each moter molecule and the ship going around every corner was soooooo cool. And the dj party was on a next level, badass ligthing and dancing was not optional every came onto the stage and just shook their bodies out.
We returned to the hotel and had our Dinner and slept.

Day 5 -

Going back:

We were told at 9:00 a.m. was the check out time and we had rush out the hotel at 8:45 a.m. and kept our bags outside and came inside to have our breakfast.
After breakfast we headed to the railway station and after making a few speeding of car souding using the floor and my suitcas wheel we boarded into the train.
Luckily this train was ok it did not have any sort of big problems. Bruhhh we are going backkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkk................

Day 6 -

Going back(part 2) -

Today was the worst one. Because when we reached Pune from Madgaon we had to literally wait 5 hours, atleast we got a place to sit. And then we caought the train and boarded in.
This train journey was possibly the worst train journey I had ever experienced at all. Literally the train's pipe had a leakage and the water from the bathroom was coming into the coach. That dirty water reached our bags and wet everything.
Anyways we had to just leave it as we cannot do anything with it. Then for Dinner we had Chicken Biryani as part of last day meal.
When I woke up I felt I had a good sleep. We ate the breakfast in the train itself and at 11:00 a.m. we reached our homes.

Conclusion

The trip had gone through very successfully with less hurdles in the way and everyone was safe and the travel guides guided everyone smoothly.
